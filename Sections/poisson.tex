This section presents the experiment and measurements of the Poissonian and Super-Poisonian probability distributions.
We followed the procedure bellow: 

\begin{itemize}
    \item Feed the CW Laser into the optical setup in figure \ref{fig:exp_lay}. 
    \item Mount Filter in between the source and the SPAD. 
    \item Define the temporal lenght of the windows integrating event counts: $100 \hspace{1mm} \mu s$ 
    \item Connect SPAD to Swabian Time Tagger. 
    \item Run the SWABIAN \texttt{tt.Counter} function and plot histogram \cite{swabian_correlation_web}. 
\end{itemize}

As shown in figure \ref{fig:photon_counts_spad1_00_1}, the coherent laser source yields a poissonian distribution even as we vary the power of the CW Laser.
Furthermore, in figure \ref{fig:photon_counts_spad1_01_0}, a polarizing beam splitter was introduced to measure the curve in low power regime; also resulting in a poissonian distribution.
The fact that the variance matches the mean ($\sigma^2 \approx \mu$), \footnote{Fano factor defined by $F = \frac{\lambda}{\sigma^2} \approx 1.0$.}, verifies the source as a coherent emitter. \\
