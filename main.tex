%Abstract 
\setabstract{This report documents the experimental 
setup for the characterization of Single Photon Avalanche Diodes (SPADs). 
The objective is to classify different light sources by performing coincidence detection experiments. 
Measurements are conducted using distinct light sources such as pulsed lasers, continous lasers, and pseudo-thermal light source. 
Throught the document, the hardware, optical setups, and results are presented to the reader.}
