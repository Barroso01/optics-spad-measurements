%Abstract 
\setabstract{This report documents the experimental characterization of Single Photon Avalanche Diodes (SPADs) 
and their application in classifying light sources via coincidence detection. 
Measurements are conducted using pulsed lasers, and pseudothermal light sources. 
Throughout the document, the hardware calibration, optical setups, and resulting photon statistics are presented.}
