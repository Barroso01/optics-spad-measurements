%Abstract 
\setabstract{This report documents the experimental characterization of Single Photon Avalanche Diodes (SPADs) and their application in classifying light sources via coincidence detection. 
Measurements utilizing pulsed lasers and pseudothermal sources extract photon statistics and validate Diffuse Correlation Spectroscopy (DCS). 
Throughout the document, hardware calibration, optical setups, and speckle decorrelation results are presented.}
